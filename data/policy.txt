SHOPPINGBOT STORE POLICIES

=== SHIPPING POLICY ===
We offer free standard shipping on all orders above ₹499 across India.
Orders below ₹499 have a flat shipping fee of ₹49.
Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days.
Express delivery (1-2 business days) is available for ₹99 extra.
We deliver to all major cities and towns across India including Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities.
Orders are shipped via trusted courier partners: Delhivery, Blue Dart, and India Post.
You will receive a tracking link via SMS and email once your order is dispatched.

=== RETURN & EXCHANGE POLICY ===
We have a 30-day easy return and exchange policy from the date of delivery.
Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags intact.
Electronics must be returned with all original accessories, manuals, and box.
To initiate a return, visit the Returns section or contact our support team.
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item.
Exchange is available for different size or color of the same product (subject to availability).
Damaged or defective products can be returned within 48 hours of delivery with photos.

=== REFUND POLICY ===
Full refund will be issued for returned items in original condition.
Refunds are credited to the original payment method.
UPI and wallet payments are refunded within 3-5 business days.
Credit/debit card refunds take 5-7 business days depending on your bank.
Cash on Delivery (COD) refunds are issued as store credits or bank transfer within 7 days.

=== PAYMENT OPTIONS ===
We accept all major payment methods:
- UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM)
- Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex)
- Debit Cards
- Net Banking (all major banks)
- EMI options available on credit cards for orders above ₹3000
- Cash on Delivery (COD) available for orders up to ₹10,000
- Buy Now Pay Later (via Simpl, ZestMoney)
All payments are 100% secure with SSL encryption.

=== WARRANTY POLICY ===
Electronics come with manufacturer warranty.
Smartphones: 1 year manufacturer warranty.
Laptops: 1 year manufacturer warranty (some brands offer 2 years).
Headphones and earphones: 6 months to 1 year warranty.
Smartwatches: 1 year manufacturer warranty.
Warranty claims must be processed through the brand's authorized service center.
We help facilitate warranty claims - contact our support team for assistance.
